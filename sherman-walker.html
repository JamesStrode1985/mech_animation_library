Sherman Walker

47 animation previews, grouped by movement and numbered in viewing order. Choose a group below to jump to it.

47 Sherman animations: heavy locomotion, moving jumps, terrain, hull and weapon control, deaths, dodges, the shoulder ram, and both dances. The left arm stays straight from shoulder to hand and moves at the shoulder. The cannon, left-hand gun, and roof gun have their own aiming motions.

Game controller setup: Actions use a stationary root at 24 fps. Supply travel in the controller and use runtime foot IK for actual terrain. Revised dodge previews show 8–9 units of travel, and the shoulder ram 10.5, over a fixed two-unit grid. These four previews play at 24 fps; most others sample at 12 fps. One-shots hold their final pose before replaying. Slopes use a 22-degree reference surface. Time Warp is an approximate choreography preview without song audio.
