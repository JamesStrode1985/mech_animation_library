HT-01 Hover Tank

47 animation previews, grouped by movement and numbered in viewing order. Choose a group below to jump to it.

47 hover-tank animations: slow hover, cruise, and boost replace walking speeds. Directional slides, moving hops, braking banks, rough-ground correction, and 18-degree slopes use the tank's rigid hover rig. Three deaths lose lift and settle onto the hull. Cannon recoil leads the body response; turret and machine gun aim separately. Dance Party and Time Warp use different hover choreography.

Game controller setup: 24 fps Actions keep ROOT stationary. The checker ground scrolls to show travel speeds; dodge previews separately show 5.5 units of controller travel and the ram 6.5. Previews sample at 8 fps, with hops, dodges, and dances at 12 fps and recoil at 24 fps. One-shots hold before replay. Add runtime hover-height sensing for real terrain. Time Warp includes no song audio. Models and editable Actions are maintained separately from this offline site.
