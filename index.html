47 animation previews, grouped by movement and numbered in viewing order. Choose a group below to jump to it.

Game controller setup: gameplay Actions retain a stationary root. Preview travel comes from a temporary controller simulation. Hill clips use 22° slopes; rough-terrain clips have roughly three times the original height variation. Terrain clips are authored examples to blend with runtime foot IK.

Weapon aiming: the cannon and each armored pod have ±20° elevation relative to the hull. Clips 36–37 show continued tracking after the hull reaches its ±5° aiming allocation; clip 38 shows independent targets. Terrain, slope, weapon-aiming, death, and dodge previews show the updated pod spacing; earlier motion previews retain their prior model revision.
